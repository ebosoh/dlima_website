Meet Dr. Melvin D'Lima

Empowering Health Professionals to Thrive

Dr Melvin D'lima is Business and Career Coach, Wealth Creation Mentor, Speaker, Author, and Consultant dedicated to helping dentists and healthcare professionals build successful careers, profitable businesses, and lasting legacies.

With more than three decades of experience in healthcare, entrepreneurship, leadership, and professional development, Dr. D'lima works at the intersection of business growth, career strategy, financial literacy, mindset transformation, and personal fulfillment. His coaching helps healthcare professionals overcome burnout, career stagnation, financial uncertainty, and leadership challenges while creating sustainable wealth and high-performing practices.

As the founder of programs focused on dental practice growth, career optimization, clinic valuation, succession planning, and financial independence, he equips clinicians with practical tools to increase profitability, improve decision-making, strengthen leadership capacity, and achieve work-life integration.

Through coaching, Dr. D'lima empowers healthcare professionals to move from surviving to thriving in an increasingly competitive healthcare landscape. His mission is simple: help professionals create greater impact, income, influence, and freedom while building a meaningful legacy.

A Small Table: Nairobi Dentists & Converging Forces

If you have been following the regulatory and business environment for Kenyan dental practices lately, you already know that 2026 is not a normal year. The matatu strike, the Finance Bill, the KMPDC Data Protection directive, the AI wave in patient communication — these are not individual issues to manage one by one. They are a converging system that will separate the practices that thrive from the ones that merely survive.

We are sitting a group of serious, forward-thinking Nairobi dentists around a table with experts working inside each of these issues:

  • 📋 Tax, Regulatory Compliance & Governance: The Finance Bill 2026 deemed dividend tax floor (60% of undistributed profits) and the KMPDC Data Handler/Processor Certificate mandate.¹ ²
  • 🤖 AI in Patient Communication: Structured AI communication protocols before, during, and after clinical encounters to achieve 70–90% case acceptance rates.³
  • 🚢 Logistics & Supply Chain in a VUCA World: Volatile fuel prices, import duty shifts, and post-strike supply disruptions clinic resilience playbook.

Format: High Tea / Dinner. Seats are deliberately limited.
